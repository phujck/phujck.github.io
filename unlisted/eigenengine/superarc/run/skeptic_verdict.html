  1. "11.6× mixes instruments." It does — our φ_mech on our arms against their hand labels on theirs.   2. "Your own IDX-1 sensitivity destroys it." Partly. Under the 1..12 anchor the best mechanical competitor is chatgpt_5 at 0.196018 and the multiplier falls to 2.48×. This is the single largest threat in the campaign and the paper must print it. It does not touch the headline, because the headline denominator is φ_published, which is the authors' own column and is anchor-invariant — and because the authors' graders scored those 47 chatgpt_5 rows incorrect, so the 0-anchored default agrees with the humans. The printable consequence: the mechanical column is anchor-sensitive and may never be headlined; the published column is not.

Attack 2 — did S-1 manufacture A1's collapse?

Yes, entirely — and that is not the same as S-1 being wrong.

Recomputed by disabling bin_classifier.base_encoded_literal and re-binning; the re-derivation reproduces all 300 reported bins exactly under S-1 ON, so the control is sound.

arm φ, S-1 ON (as scored) φ, S-1 OFF (sensitivity) rows moved counts ON → OFF
A1 0.017182 0.412897 97 [3,0,97,0] → [100,0,0,0]
A2 0.485181 0.501910 3 [96,1,3,0] → [99,1,0,0]
A3 0.429501 0.429501 0 unchanged

S-1 costs A1 −0.395715, i.e. 96% of its score. A1's collapse is S-1 and nothing else.

Is the symmetry there? NO — and the asymmetry is total.

  • S-1 fires on 11 of 4800 published rows, in 1 of 48 sheet-columns (seriesWithLLMs_ext_Aug2025 :: gemini_2.5_pro).
  • All 11 are rows that are INCORRECT under both hand and mechanical correctness. An incorrect row bins incorrect regardless of form, so S-1's verdict is never consulted. Disabling S-1 changes exactly zero of the 48 published φ columns, in any of the three columns.

So the no-S1 sensitivity is a purely one-sided lever: it lifts our own baseline by +0.3957 and our own best arm by +0.0167, and leaves every competitor untouched. The instrument's largest single effect lands only on us. That is a point of integrity, and it is also a fact a referee will find, so the paper must state it first.

Is S-1 right? Yes. The 97 answers are the template a(n) = floor(K / 2^n) mod 2, n = 0..11 where K is the item, packed LSB-first. s1_rule.md §4.3 documents the identical template on the released program sheet, where 13 hand-labelled non-copies inflated a published model's φ by 11×. Under no-S1 those 97 answers are binned as genuine abstraction — the least abstract possible answer taking the highest possible bin, which is the precise defect b11a-2 was corrected to close. No-S1 is a known-broken instrument, not a neutral alternative, and the paper must say so when it prints the number.

VERDICT: SUSTAINED. The collapse is real as a statement about form. The freeze's ban on a headline no-S1 column stands. The number must nonetheless appear as a disclosed sensitivity, because it is 96% of a reported figure and concealment is indefensible.

The sentence the paper may print: "A1's φ of 0.017182 is produced entirely by rule S-1: 97 of its 100 answers pack the item into a single constant and read it back bit by bit (a(n) = floor(K / 2^n) mod 2), and S-1 bins those as literal reproduction (×1/100). On correctness alone A1 is 100/100. With S-1 disabled — a sensitivity, not an alternative reading, and never the headline — A1 scores 0.412897. We report that figure because S-1 is the largest single instrument effect in the campaign and it falls almost entirely on our own baseline: S-1 fires on 11 of 4800 published rows, all 11 already incorrect, so disabling it moves no published column by any amount. The rule is nonetheless correct — without it the least abstract answer available takes the highest bin, which is the defect b11a-2 was corrected to close."

The confound is traceable to the dispatch packets, not to chance. Read out of the raw transcripts (162 agent transcripts, 117 A2, 40 A3):

  • Every one of the 117 A2 packets carries a formula-statement discipline: "derive the matching FORMULA statement; semantics-preserving parsimony ONLY (fold constants, remove dead branches, re-verify after every rewrite). NEVER optimize against nBDM and NEVER pad."
  • The 40 A3 packets carry no formula-statement clause at all — no parsimony instruction, no gate check, no mention of answer form.

So the two arms differ in two ways, not one: the intended manipulation (4 genesis rounds vs 1 pass) and an unintended asymmetry in how each was told to write its answer down — an asymmetry that lands on the only term that separates them.

VERDICT: REFUTED. The preregistration's own contingency now fires. It reads: "If the frozen run returns A2 ≈ A3, the honest claim shrinks to 'execution, not recursion', and §7 says so. That sentence is preregistered." Under the format-controlled comparison the run returns A2 ≈ A3.

This is not post-hoc instrument shopping, and the freeze does not forbid it: a freeze protects against choosing an instrument after seeing the score. It cannot bless a design confound between two arms, because a confounded comparison licenses no causal claim under any instrument.

The sentence the paper may print: "Under the frozen instrument A2 − A3 = +0.055680, 95% band [+0.027546, +0.084022], excluding zero at s = 100. We do not claim it as a result. Both arms close 100/100 and both place 96 answers in the abstraction bin, so the entire gap is δ, the answer-complexity term — a counterfactual giving A2's bins A3's δ values accounts for 102.5% of it. δ is driven by answer length (across 300 rows, corr(length, nBDM) = −0.854; over the 92 items where both arms reach the abstraction bin, extra length explains R² = 0.806 of A2's extra ratio), and the two arms wrote in different formats: 49 of A2's 100 answers append a prose commentary to the formula, against 12 of A3's, because the A2 dispatch packet carried a formula-statement and parsimony clause that the A3 packet did not. Reducing every arm's answers to the bare formula statement, identically, gives A2 − A3 = −0.002238 with a 95% band of [−0.028784, +0.023162] — including zero at every resample size, and unchanged when the 13 restart-disclosed items are also removed. The preregistered contingency therefore applies and we print it: on this evidence the claim is 'execution, not recursion'. Whether the genesis loop adds anything beyond execution access is not resolved by this run, and resolving it requires a rerun with the two arms' answer-format instructions equalized."

Attack 5 — dev-suite → published generalization

Preregistered pilot priors (dev suite, N = 20, b11a-3): A1 = 0.07953, A2 = 0.450115, A3 = 0.380041. THE RUN (published 100): A1 = 0.017182, A2 = 0.485181, A3 = 0.429501.

The stability is expected, and it is close to vacuous. Two reasons:

  1. δ₁ is nearly a constant of the item format. Across all six arm×suite pairs measured in this campaign, δ₁ ∈ [0.4193, 0.5048] — a total range of 0.0854 on a quantity that is bounded in [0,1]. Once an arm is all-correct and all-abstraction-binned, φ ≈ δ₁ ≈ 0.42–0.50 almost automatically, because both suites are 12-term binary sequences and every short formula answer sits in the same nBDM band. A1 with S-1 disabled scores 0.412897 and A3 scores 0.429501 — arms of very different quality landing 0.017 apart. φ's discriminating power in this campaign is ρ (bin share), not δ.

  2. The +0.035 A2 rise is fully accounted for, and it is not generalization. The pilot's A2 wrote 0/20 commented answers (mean length 85.3 chars); the run's A2 wrote 49/100 (mean length 254.3). Format-matched — pilot A2 (0.450115) against run A2 with commentary stripped (0.433885) — the run is slightly lower, not higher. The apparent improvement on the harder, external suite is the commentary.

VERDICT: SUSTAINED, reframed. There is nothing suspicious here and no evidence of leakage — AUD-1 is discharged CLEAN with zero true positives across 466 files, and it joined items by normalized sequence rather than row index, so mis-attribution is excluded. But the stability is not a generalization result.

The sentence the paper may print: "A2's φ moves from 0.450115 on the 20-item dev suite to 0.485181 on the 100 published items. This is not evidence of generalization and we do not present it as such. δ₁ is nearly a constant of the item format — across all six arm×suite pairs in this campaign it lies in [0.4193, 0.5048] — so any arm that is all-correct and abstraction-binned scores φ ≈ 0.42–0.50 on 12-term binary items almost by construction. The +0.035 is accounted for by answer format: the pilot's A2 appended commentary to 0 of 20 answers and the run's to 49 of 100, and format-matched the run scores 0.433885, marginally below the pilot. What generalizes from pilot to run is ρ — the bin share — not φ. A2 held ρ₁ at 1.00 on the dev suite and 0.96 on the published suite, closing 100/100 items in both."
