Many worlds.

07 · ASSEMBLING ATLAS

The same object,
at every scale.

The theory, the exact GPU implementation and the language experiments now meet at a specific engineering requirement: the relations and operations needed by a question must survive ingestion, just as retained modes survive an upfold.

Ingest the relations

Represent who, what, when and why with shared source identity. Preserve qualifications and conflicting observations.

Grade the whole object

Transport internal distinctions, effective law and decoder together. Use the residual to decide when another section is needed.

Reconstruct the enquiry

Recover all required witnesses, perform the requested comparison or aggregation, and return the original evidence.

The gain is already concrete for correlated states: exact answers through a small reusable continuation. The language tests tell us where that same discipline must be applied next. Counting projects requires the project relation and the counting operation, as well as passages about projects.
