I work on representation.

A theory carves the world into categories, keeps them in symbols, and discards everything except the distinctions its predictions need. That is a physical act. The symbols occupy matter, the discarding has a price, and a theory built by throwing detail away ends up thermodynamic whether or not it started that way. Open systems, control, Koopman mechanics, superoscillations, reservoir computing and imaginary-time methods are one problem worked in six settings, and the sum is a thermodynamics of representation.

What I work on

An environment is a representation of its influence

Trace out a harmonic bath exactly and what remains is the system driven by coloured noise: a closed, exact equation in which the whole of the environment survives as the statistics of a few random fields. The noise is derived by a two-time Hubbard-Stratonovich transformation rather than postulated, the construction holds from an equilibrated start, and its classical limit is the generalised Langevin equation. The mean-force Hamiltonian is the equilibrium form of the same statement: the bath as imaginary-time noise, and the operator form of the reduced state as an inverse representability problem.

Identity of response is not identity of system

Tracking control inverts the usual question: given the trajectory an observable should follow, solve for the field. The equation is nonlinear and explicit, and it lets one many-body system reproduce another's optical spectrum. The limit of the idea is the twinning field, a single pulse under which two distinct materials are optically indistinguishable, and for generic pairs it exists. Non-uniqueness of the driving field, the distinguishability of Mott insulators in time against frequency, and the sequential silencing of a mixture's components are the same fact in three guises.

Classical and quantum mechanics differ by an algebraic operation

Koopman and von Neumann put classical mechanics on a Hilbert space with commuting position and momentum. Once both theories share a space their difference is locatable: a commutator that is present or absent, a term the minimal-coupling rule carries on one side only. The wave operator, the square root of the density matrix, unifies Hilbert space, Wigner phase space and Koopman mechanics with positivity automatic and the Wigner function as a phase-space wavefunction, and the free particle and the harmonic oscillator are one unitary and a change of clock apart. Observables in Motion is the textbook treatment.

A signal can carry more than its bandwidth

A band-limited wave can oscillate locally faster than any of its Fourier components. The mechanism is destructive interference arranged on purpose, there is a constructive recipe for it from arbitrary waveforms, and it has been realised with terahertz fields. Pointed at a resonance beyond the component bandwidth it buys an order of magnitude in spectroscopic sensitivity and more, and it confines a condensate below the wavelength of the light that traps it.

Superspectroscopy · Super-sensing · Condensate confinement

Computation is a question of dimensionality

Encode the input in the Hamiltonian of a few qubits, evolve for a fixed time and read out local expectations: the result is a feature map, and a linear readout over it regresses, synthesises and forecasts. The reservoir has no memory, so temporal dependence is reconstructed at the readout from delayed copies. The capacity comes from the dimension of the function space the dynamics spans rather than from entanglement or recurrence, which is why less machinery is more. Unwrapping a photonic reservoir's phase past its natural period buys the same thing through synthetic frequencies, and a single atom under high-harmonic generation is, in this sense, a computer.

Resolution is a resource

Imaginary time is the dynamics of thermalisation, and a unitary device can emulate it: a binomially weighted sum of overlaps between a state run forwards and backwards in real time is the imaginary-time propagator exactly, and one trajectory of measurements yields the ground state and the spectrum. The cost is in the shots. Resolving the smallest gap while staying stable against the largest costs exponentially in their ratio, which is where the hardness of ground-state estimation lives. Coarse-grained spectral information at a precision set by the local gaps is polynomial. There are no free lunches, but there are free snacks.

A thermodynamic theory of representation

Each of these prices something: the statistics a bath is reduced to, the field that makes two systems one, the operation that separates two mechanics, the bandwidth a wave exceeds, the dimension a reservoir spans, the shots a spectrum costs. The Cost of Complexity makes the price the object of study. A representation must be embodied, the apparatus that organises it must itself be represented, and under additive composition with a positive price per nat the efficient register grows logarithmically; maximum entropy at fixed mean cost gives the Zipf family, and the mean log-rank of a memory's contents bounds the heat its erasure must release. The Phases of Hierarchy is the dynamical half: a register revised by the population it describes. Aggregation sharpens with number, a sharp enough response flips the loop, layers attenuate it at a cost in delay, and depth becomes a phase variable with a flat unstable phase, a frozen inertial phase and a logarithmic window between.
