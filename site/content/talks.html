Nonlinear Computing with Optical Twinning Trees

Two different materials, one common field, one identical optical response. The talk turns that equality into a physical root condition, lets feedback acquire the root, and then branches the construction into an optical twinning tree that finds all four roots of a factorised quartic.

  • The scalar delay boundary predicts the useful many-body gain window.
  • A Kerr pair encodes and solves a quadratic without a digital root solver.
  • Three cavities and seven real variables acquire four quartic roots.
