EigenEngine

An integrated research environment for people and AI agents.

By the time an argument reaches the page, most of what made it trustworthy has been discarded. EigenEngine keeps it as a research object, a graph of linked records of which the paper is one reading. This page is itself such a graph, and every card in it opens in the same way.

  1. The research object

    Research begins with an idea, which has to be sharpened into claims before anyone can argue with it. Each claim rests on premises, a derivation, a passage in the literature, a calculation or a figure. We call the linked whole a research object. A record in it that holds other records, and carries named links on its outside, is a conceptric.

    The research object on its own page

  2. Navigating conceptrics

    By query diffusion: a question places weight on the records it matches, the weight spreads along their links, each container reports its total before it is opened, and the search descends only into the heaviest.

  3. From conceptrics to papers

    A paper or a talk is a projection of the graph into a line for one reader, from what they already know to the result.

  4. Keeping it consistent

    A change marks its neighbours for review, and each review stops it or passes it on, which is how a renamed term reaches every sentence that uses it.
