EigenEngine

Eigen is a store that AI agents, the programs that answer questions and do tasks on a person's behalf, work through instead of reading documents directly. It keeps what they read, limits what each agent may open, and attaches to every answer the passages it came from. Pre-release. eigen@gmccaul.co.uk

How it works

Persistence, permission, provenance

Persistence

Documents are stored once, as a record of passages: what each says, the names, dates and numbers in it, and its links to other passages. The next question starts from the record instead of reading everything again.

Permission

An agent reaches the record only through the index, and opens only the branches its question needs. Branches the owner has not permitted it to read are never opened. Eigen runs on the owner's own machine; the documents do not leave it.

Provenance

Every answer carries the passages it used and a fingerprint of each. Anyone with the sources can follow the chain back, and if a passage has changed since, the answer is flagged as no longer backed.

Benchmark

SuperARC

A published test gives a model one hundred short sequences of ones and zeros and asks for the rule behind each. Eigen ran every proposed rule before answering with it. 96 of its 100 answers were real rules; the models in the published results mostly wrote the sequence back out.
