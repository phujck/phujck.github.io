From conceptrics to papers

A research object is a graph, but it is read one record at a time. The right order depends on the reader, whom we describe by a receiver state: what they already know. A record can be read once the state holds all it needs, and reading adds to the state. A paper or a talk is a path of such steps to the result: a projection of the object.
