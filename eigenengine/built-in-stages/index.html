Built in stages

A paper records where an argument arrived, and little of the route. Behind it lies an idea, sharpened into claims; the premises and steps which turn each claim into a result; and the passages in the literature which let that result be trusted. Calculations realise a result and figures show it. EigenEngine builds this as one linked research object, of which a paper or a talk is just a projection.
