As my reading partner, first state the central argument in one sentence. For each objection ask how relevant it is to that argument. If it were granted in full, what would change? It is entirely acceptable to find no material objection. There is no quota, and novelty, familiarity or a missing measurement on its own settles neither validity nor importance.

For every objection you report, including one already answered, start its entry on a new line with an ID such as “O1.”. Include these three labels in that entry:
Relevance: name the precise inference, hypothesis, regime or interpretation it reaches. Apply a deletion test: quote or locate the disputed statement, remove or narrow it, and trace an actual dependency. Which derivation, construction or main consequence changes? If only the interpretation in that sentence changes, call it a local remark. A short sentence can still be central if a result uses it. Check whether the proposed case is already represented by a general definition or a classified regime.
Importance: give the size of the change to the paper’s central contribution if the objection holds. Weigh the change in the paper’s answer to its main question. If the objection changes only a passing remark, record that local effect once. It can receive zero weight for the central argument. Ease of spotting a sentence and time spent debating it supply no extra weight.
Physical witness: does any physical system exhibit the property used in the counterexample under the same resource, task, fidelity, scale and accounting for the apparatus? Give the smallest witness, name an observed system and checked primary source where available, or distinguish a physically justified model, a formal example and an unknown. Explain what a finite measurement can establish about a scaling claim. A valid formal counterexample can defeat a theorem even when its relevance to actual devices is unestablished. State what evidence would settle an open physical question.

Give the best textual answer, rank the remaining effects by relevance and importance, and distribute 100 impact points, including a reserve for damage you have not established. Give every reported ID a share, including zero where appropriate. Avoid double-counting, and explain genuine ties. These are comparative judgements about this argument. End with a line in the form “Weights: O1=…, O2=…, reserve=…” using numbers totalling 100. If there are no objections, give a short account of what you checked and end with “Weights: reserve=100”.
