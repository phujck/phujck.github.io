Appendix G — Scale-dependent erasure costs

A separate physical identification is possible when an integer vv names a register uniformly occupied over vv distinguishable, equal-entropy states. Reset then removes lnv\ln v nats. For independent uniform component registers,

H=ln(∏k=1nvk)=∑k=1nlnvk.H=\ln\!\left(\prod_{k=1}^n v_k\right)=\sum_{k=1}^n\ln v_k. (G1)

Correlated components instead contribute their joint entropy. This model connects multiplicative refinement to additive reset cost through the occupied volume, rather than the arithmetic size of a witness.

Within the uniform-reset model, homogeneity is an assumption about the refinement protocol. Landauer’s principle bounds mean heat per removed nat from below by kBTk_BT [20]. An implementation can pay more, and the overhead can vary with depth. A register that already supports mm distinctions may need further apparatus to hold a refinement of them apart, for instance additional degrees of freedom or wider energy gaps, and that overhead can depend on mm.
