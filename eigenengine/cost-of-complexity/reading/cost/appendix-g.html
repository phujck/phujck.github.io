Appendix G — Maximum-entropy rank ensembles

With fixed rank labels, normalisation and a mean cost ℓ\ell, the entropy Lagrangian is

∂∂p(r)[S[p]−α(∑p(r)−1)−λ(∑p(r)logr−ℓ)]=0\frac{\partial}{\partial p(r)}\left[ S[p] - \alpha\bigl(\sum p(r)-1\bigr) - \lambda\bigl(\sum p(r)\log r - \ell\bigr) \right] = 0 (G1)

Its stationary condition is −lnp(r)−1−α−λlnr=0-\ln p(r)-1-\alpha-\lambda\ln r=0. Normalisation gives Eq. (46). Strict concavity of entropy gives the unique maximiser for an interior feasible mean. The derivative dC/dλ=−Varλ(lnr)<0dC/d\lambda=-\operatorname{Var}_{\lambda}(\ln r)<0 for finite R≥2R\ge2 and finite λ\lambda gives uniqueness of the multiplier. The endpoint ensembles follow as λ→±∞\lambda\to\pm\infty. If ranks must also follow decreasing occupation probability, λ≥0\lambda\ge0 and the maximum mean is the uniform value ln(R!)/R\ln(R!)/R.

For the scale-covariant cost of Eq. (D5), the same calculation gives

pα(r∣λ)=1ZR,α(λ)exp[−λrα−1α].p_\alpha(r\mid\lambda) = \frac{1}{Z_{R,\alpha}(\lambda)} \exp\!\left[-\lambda\,\frac{r^{\alpha}-1}{\alpha}\right]. (G2)

with the logarithmic limit at α=0\alpha=0. For α>0\alpha>0 and λ>0\lambda>0 the large-rank weights decay as exp[−(λ/α)rα]\exp[-(\lambda/\alpha)r^\alpha], giving finite normalisation and mean cost. Within this exact scale-covariant family, the logarithmic member has the finite positive normalisation boundary discussed in the main text.
