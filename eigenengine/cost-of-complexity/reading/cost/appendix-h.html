Appendix H — Critical response of the rank ensemble

For ε=λ−1>0\varepsilon=\lambda-1>0, ζ(1+ε)=ε−1+O(1)\zeta(1+\varepsilon)=\varepsilon^{-1}+O(1), with its usual differentiable Laurent expansion. Consequently

C(1+ε)=ε−1+O(1),χ(1+ε)=ε−2+O(1).C(1+\varepsilon)=\varepsilon^{-1}+O(1),\qquad \chi(1+\varepsilon)=\varepsilon^{-2}+O(1). (H1)

These are infinite-vocabulary asymptotics. At finite cutoff and λ=1\lambda=1, integral comparison gives, for j=0,1,2j=0,1,2,

Aj(R):=∑r=1R(lnr)jr=(lnR)j+1j+1+O(1).A_j(R):=\sum_{r=1}^R\frac{(\ln r)^j}{r} =\frac{(\ln R)^{j+1}}{j+1}+O(1). (H2)

The errors are bounded because each summand is eventually decreasing and integrable on any fixed initial interval. With L=lnRL=\ln R,

CR(1)=A1/A0=12L+O(1),χR(1)=A2/A0−(A1/A0)2=112L2+O(L),H(pR)=CR(1)+lnA0=12L+lnL+O(1).\begin{aligned} C_R(1)&=A_1/A_0=\tfrac12L+O(1),\\ \chi_R(1)&=A_2/A_0-(A_1/A_0)^2 =\tfrac1{12}L^2+O(L),\\ H(p_R)&=C_R(1)+\ln A_0 =\tfrac12L+\ln L+O(1). \end{aligned} (H3)

Finite devices can occupy this ensemble. Divergent heat per complete reset is a statement about the limit R→∞R\to\infty under the reset assumptions of Eq. (38).
