The proof of Theorem 2 uses only monotonicity and multiplicative additivity.

Proof. The function gg defined in Eq. (18) is nondecreasing, satisfies g(1)=0g(1)=0, and obeys Eq. (19). For any fixed n≥2n\ge2 and positive integer kk, set

ak:=⌊klognlog2⌋.a_k := \left\lfloor k\frac{\log n}{\log 2} \right\rfloor. (A1)

2ak≤nk<2ak+1.2^{a_k}\le n^k<2^{a_k+1}. (A2)

akg(2)≤kg(n)≤(ak+1)g(2).a_k g(2) \le k g(n) \le (a_k+1)g(2). (A3)
