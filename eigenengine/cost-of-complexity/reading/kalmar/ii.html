II. Setting

We use the notation of Ref. [1]. A register of range N distinguishes N alternatives. For an organisation R, Bℛ(N) is the budget sufficient to realise any of the first N symbols in typicality order, and

Kreg(N)=infℛ∈𝒜NBℛ(N).K_{\mathrm{reg}}(N)=\inf_{\mathcal R\in\mathcal A_N} B_{\mathcal R}(N). (1)

When two registers can implement a positional choice at the sum of their budgets,

Kreg(mn)≤Kreg(m)+Kreg(n),K_{\mathrm{reg}}(mn)\le K_{\mathrm{reg}}(m)+K_{\mathrm{reg}}(n), (2)

and the limit κ∞ = limN→∞ Kreg(N)/ ln N exists, with

κ∞lnN≤Kreg(N).\kappa_\infty\ln N\le K_{\mathrm{reg}}(N). (3)

Under homogeneous refinement,

Kreg(n)=K0+κlnn,K_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)=K_0+\kappa\ln n, (4)

where K₀ = Kreg(1) is the existence cost. The shared cost of two registers is then IK(m:n) = Kreg(m) + Kreg(n) − Kreg(mn) = K₀. We write k₀ = K₀/κ and measure budgets in units of κ.

With symbol cost ln r at rank r, maximum entropy at fixed mean cost gives the Zipf family on a vocabulary of size R,

p(r∣λ)=r−λZR(λ),ZR(λ)=∑r=1Rr−λ,p(r\mid\lambda)=\frac{r^{-\lambda}}{Z_R(\lambda)},\qquad Z_R(\lambda)=\sum_{r=1}^{R}r^{-\lambda}, (5)

with Z∞(λ) = ζ(λ) for λ > 1. Two properties of this family are used below. Write CR(λ)=∑rp(r∣λ)lnrC_R(\lambda)=\sum_r p(r\mid\lambda)\ln r for the mean cost and L = ln R for the capacity.

Proposition 1 (Pinning of the rank multiplier). For fixed real x,

limR→∞CR(1+x/L)L=c(x):=1x−1ex−1,\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{C_R(1+x/L)}{L}=c(x):=\frac1x-\frac{1}{e^x-1}, (6)

with c(0) = 1/2. The function c decreases from one to zero. If the mean cost is a fixed fraction c ∈ (0, 1) of the capacity, then λR → 1 and (λR − 1) ln R → x(c), the solution of c(x) = c.

In the coordinate u = ln r there are eu ranks of cost at most u, so the family is a truncated exponential on [0, L] with rate λ − 1. At λ = 1 the weight exp(−λu) cancels the density of ranks. This is the linear entropy of Ref. [11], and a related asymptotic statement is given in Ref. [12].

Any λ > 1 has cost density zero and any λ < 1 has cost density one. Only λ = 1+O(1/ ln R) uses an intermediate fraction of the capacity.

Proposition 2 (Redundancy of the logarithmic cost). Let K(r) = K₀ +κ ln r on {1, …, R}, let h(r) = 1/(r HR) be the harmonic distribution with HR=∑r≤R1/rH_R=\sum_{r\le R}1/r, and let p be any distribution on the same set. Then

⟨K⟩p−κH(p)=(K0−κlnHR)+κD(p‖h),\langle K\rangle_p-\kappa H(p)=\bigl(K_0-\kappa\ln H_R\bigr)+\kappa D(p\Vert h), (7)

where H(p) is the Shannon entropy and D the relative entropy in nats.

The first bracket is non-negative when the costs are the lengths of a prefix code, by the Kraft inequality [13]. Among occupations of a logarithmically priced register, the harmonic member λ = 1 is the only one for which the mean cost equals the entropy carried, up to that bracket.

A self-delimiting register on R ranks has

K0≥κlnHR=κlnlnR+O(1/lnR).K_0\ge\kappa\ln H_R=\kappa\ln\ln R+O(1/\ln R). (8)
