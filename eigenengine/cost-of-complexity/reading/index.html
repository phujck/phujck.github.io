What does it cost to hold a distinction once the machinery that gives you access to it belongs in the same physical account? Ask for a forecast, then for the explanation the proofs support.

Read the paper in two messages

A model can approach a synthesis with a deflationary prior, expecting it to reduce to familiar work. A referee-style prompt can reinforce that expectation. The model may import an assumption from another field and criticise a claim the author never made. For a proposed foundation, the question is how much follows from how few physical assumptions. Why ask for a forecast first?

A foundational paper is a claim about the economy of an explanation. A result may be familiar in thermodynamics or information theory and still play a different role when it follows from a physical account of the apparatus. A reader who substitutes the familiar derivation can overlook a consequence or attack a premise used by only one theorem. The first prompt records what the reader expects to need. The second asks it to trace each consequence back to what the proof uses and judge an objection by what would change if it held.

Research on framing and evidence
  • With the research ideas held fixed, changing the information a judge received about other reviewers’ novelty assessments could change its verdict. Crucially, the same cue helped some judges and hurt others. Sternlicht et al., Old Ideas, Novel Problems ↗
  • Changing only a title’s style shifted scores for otherwise identical generated abstracts. In another experiment, telling an assistant that a user liked or disliked a piece of writing shifted the tone of its feedback. Du, TitleTrap ↗ · Sharma et al., Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models ↗
  • Four models differed substantially in their recommendations on 200 already-published articles and in their detection of deliberately planted flaws. That gives a reader a reason to ask which particular inference a criticism invalidates. Le Guellec et al., Major Revision, Minor Accountability ↗
  • A video study compared responses across clips when relevant evidence was preserved, altered or removed. It offers a methodological analogy for checking what a judgement depends on. Its experiments measured video reasoning. Kang et al., Behavior Pack Optimization for Video MLLM Post-Training ↗

These studies motivate the separation. Keeping the first reply lets you inspect how this reader updated its view. The exercise cannot certify that the result is unbiased.
