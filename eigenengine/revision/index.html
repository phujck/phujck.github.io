Keeping it consistent

What happens when something changes?

No record in a research object stands alone: an edit to one can falsify others nobody touched. Each record is therefore a state machine: current, needs review or changed. When one changes, those written against it are marked, and stay marked until someone has looked. A review stops the change or passes it on. A renamed term is thereby forced through the conceptric until no record uses the old word.
